# 1864-D Shared-Foot Motion Student: Offline Distillation Round 1

Date: 2026-07-29

## Outcome

Round 1 is complete and is **rejected for deployment**.  The network,
multi-seed data, frozen Teacher labeling path, checkpoints, ONNX export, and
closed-loop evaluation are all reproducible.  Offline imitation improved, but
the fully distilled Student suffered covariate shift in closed-loop Isaac Lab
rollouts.  No real-robot policy configuration was changed.

## Closed-loop result

Isaac Lab, seed 161803, 64 environments, 300 steps, five frictions and two
commands:

- Estimated fall/reset events: 97 across 640 condition-environment rollouts
- Low-friction, 1.0 m/s command mean speed: 0.201 m/s
- High-friction, 1.0 m/s command mean speed: 0.259 m/s
- Maximum matrix mean lateral displacement: 0.160 m

The Student preserved low-friction slowing but lost high-friction command
tracking and stability.  The root cause is one-shot behavior cloning from
states generated by the safe rollout policy: small action errors compound after
the Student moves off that state distribution.

## Required next round

1. Roll out the Round-1 Student in simulation and collect the states it
   actually visits, including recovery/fall precursors.
2. Label those states with the frozen Oracle Teacher.
3. Add them to the current three-seed corpus; do not replace the safe data.
4. Use a staged trust region: first fit the shared foot encoder and final actor
   head, then unfreeze the whole actor with a small learning rate.
5. Select by closed-loop fall rate and speed/friction behavior, not offline
   action MAE alone.
6. Keep the current deploy configuration unchanged until Isaac Lab and MuJoCo
   both pass.

# Flexible magnetic foot: real Hall boundary, canonical layout and wire schema

## Non-negotiable measurement boundary

The real source of truth is `/home/mosense/guo_1/vola_sensor`.  One physical
foot directly provides only:

- raw Hall counts `hall_xyz[15,3]` in wire XYZ order;
- temperature `temperature_c[15]`;
- host receive timestamp, host/parser sequence and frame validity.

It does **not** provide normal force, tangential force, contact pressure,
friction coefficient or magnetic values in tesla.  Baseline subtraction,
temperature compensation, filtering and per-channel scaling remain magnetic
signal preprocessing; they do not turn the signal into force.  The dashboard's
heat, load, centre and component values are norms/weighted statistics of Hall
response and must not be logged or labelled as physical force/COP.

## Coordinate and point order

The canonical Hall frame is the matching G1 `ankle_roll_link` local frame:

- `+x`: toward the toe;
- `+y`: toward the robot's left;
- `+z`: upward;
- magnetic order: `[left/right, P00..P14, X/Y/Z]`;
- real raw unit: device counts;
- simulation magnetic unit: tesla before observation normalization;
- policy unit: dimensionless, per-channel baseline/temperature compensated
  and scaled independently for real and simulated domains.

The physical Hall sites are `P0` through `P14`, never a reshaped 3-by-5 or
5-by-3 array:

```text
forefoot:        P0
            P1   P2   P3
                 P4

midfoot:         P5
            P6   P7   P8
                 P9

heel:            P10
            P11  P12  P13
                 P14
```

`PROVISIONAL_NORMALIZED_LAYOUT` in
`unitree_rl_lab.traction.sensor_layout` is the only software configuration for
these positions, region IDs, BLE mapping, chip-axis transforms, and right-foot
mirror transform. The positions preserve the supplied topology and nonuniform
relative spacing. They were digitized from the 1280×1793 pixel 1:1 A4
`vola_sensor/2.png` using the drawn sole bounds `u=7..508, v=488..1780`, then
stored as `normalized_sole_length_width_from_a4_scan`. The square drawing marks
are approximately 4 mm across. These scan-derived centres remain subject to a
final PCB/CAD coordinate measurement; no Hall-to-force calibration is implied.

The current BLE mapping is the explicit provisional identity
`channel 0..14 -> P00..P14`. The chip-local XY rotations are the rotations used
by the current visualization. Both this mapping/sign convention and the
right-foot mirror transform must be validated with signed three-axis magnetic
motion tests.  It cannot be validated by assuming an applied force direction.

## Current BLE wire format

- advertised device name: `FootSensor15`;
- notify characteristic: `0000ab01-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb`;
- frame length: 125 bytes;
- accepted header: byte 0 `0x7D`, byte 2 `0xF0`, byte 3 `0x02`;
- data: bytes `[4,124)`, 15 channels, 8 bytes per channel;
- channel format: big-endian signed int16 `T_x10, Hall_X, Hall_Y, Hall_Z`;
- final byte: reserved/undocumented;
- timestamps and sequence numbers: generated by the host;
- Hall unwrap bound: ±2,000,000 counts;
- plausible temperature display range in the existing UI: −40 to 125 °C.

The parser does not claim a device sampling rate. It measures arrival times at
runtime because no stable rate or device timestamp is encoded in the known
frame.

## Preprocessing and calibration status

No measured counts-to-tesla transfer function and no model mapping either

`Hall[15,3] -> point force`

or

`Hall[15,3] -> calibrated net [Fx,Fy,Fz]`

was found in the local sensor project.  The Hall frame therefore has no force
field or Hall-to-force callable.  `SingleFootSensorAdapter` performs only
channel and axis ordering.  `DualFootSensorAggregator.magnetic_input()` emits
`hall_xyz[2,15,3]`, `temperature_c[2,15]`, per-foot validity, age, measured
period and sequence; it never copies one foot into the other and exposes no
normal/tangential-force API.

The real-time F0M1 bridge may subtract a per-channel unloaded baseline, apply
temperature compensation and divide by a per-channel response scale.  Its
result is explicitly dimensionless normalized Hall data.  Simulation retains
tesla internally, then maps simulated baseline-relative Hall values into the
same dimensionless policy range.  There is no implied counts-to-tesla or
Hall-to-newton conversion between the two paths.

The canonical real-data specification and current collection-quality status are
maintained in
`/home/mosense/guo_1/vola_sensor/真实足底传感器数据格式.md`.
Raw diagnostic/session data now exist, but the 2026-08-09 walking session failed
the dual-foot availability gate because the left link averaged only about
42.97 Hz with repeated reconnects.  No accepted `normalization/left.json` and
`normalization/right.json` were found at the 2026-08-11 audit.  Therefore the
recordings are useful for link diagnostics, but they do not authorize inventing
baseline, temperature coefficients, channel scales, axis signs or a deployable
F0M1 normalization.

The software accepts two separately identified feet, but no physical dual-foot
symmetry claim is made until both BLE addresses, channel order and signed axes
are measured.
